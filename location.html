Why do we evaluate candidate testing sites on Earth?

Sending a rover or an astronaut to the Moon is extremely high risk. Testing navigation software, spacesuit joints, and power systems in Earth's harshest volcanic plateaus and caves ensures equipment works reliably before launch!

ATC-CHJ-5050 • TERRESTRIAL TESTING SITE

Atacama Desert (ALMA Chajnantor)

Driest non-polar region on Earth. The 66 radio antennas of ALMA operate at 5,050 meters altitude where atmospheric water vapor drops below 0.1 mm.
