What is an Earth Analog?

An "Earth Analog" is a location on our planet whose geology, climate, or rock chemistry mirrors another world. Chile’s dry Atacama Desert mimics volcanic lunar plains, and Spanish lava caves mirror subsurface moon shelters!

Why Do Space Agencies Use Them?

Launching rockets is risky and costs billions. NASA, ESA, and JAXA test robot rover wheels, pressurized spacesuits, drilling tools, and life support systems in Earth's extreme deserts and caves to prevent fatal mission failures.

🌍 OUR HOME PLANET

Planet Earth

1.00g Normal Gravity

A vibrant blue marble covered with deep oceans, an oxygen-rich atmosphere that sustains life, and an active magnetic field that shields us from harmful solar radiation.

Equatorial Radius (Size) 6,378 km Nearly 4× wider than Moon
Surface Gravity 9.81 m/s² (1.00g) Our normal Earth weight
Planetary Mass 5.97 × 10²⁴ kg 81× heavier than the Moon
Speed to Reach Space 11.2 km/s (40,300 km/h) Escape velocity threshold
Atmosphere 78% Nitrogen, 21% Oxygen Full breathable air pressure
Natural Radiation Shield Low (2.4 mSv/year) Protected by magnetic field
Axial Tilt (Seasons) 23.44° Gives Earth summer & winter
Day Length 24 Hours One full day/night rotation
🌕 NATURAL SATELLITE

The Moon

0.165g (1/6th Gravity)

A timeless, crater-covered world with no air and no weather. Because there is no wind or water erosion, footprints left by Apollo astronauts over 50 years ago remain untouched.

Mean Radius (Size) 1,737 km (27% of Earth) Roughly the width of Australia
Surface Gravity 1.62 m/s² (0.165g) You weigh 6× less here!
Lunar Mass 7.34 × 10²² kg (1.2%) Light celestial body
Speed to Reach Space 2.38 km/s (8,570 km/h) Much easier to launch rockets!
Atmosphere Near-Total Vacuum No air to breathe or trap heat
Space Radiation High (380–420 mSv/yr) Astronauts need shielded bases
Axial Tilt 1.54° Permanent shadows in craters
Day Length 29.5 Earth Days 2 weeks of sun, 2 weeks night
