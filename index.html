🌍 OUR HOME PLANET

Planet Earth

1.00g Normal Gravity

A vibrant planetary body covered with liquid oceans, an oxygen-rich atmosphere sustaining active hydrological cycles, and a convective geomagnetic dynamo shielding the surface from cosmic rays.

Equatorial Radius 6,378 km Nearly 4× wider than Moon
Surface Gravity 9.81 m/s² (1.00g) Standard terrestrial baseline
Planetary Mass 5.97 × 10²⁴ kg 81.3× heavier than Moon
Escape Velocity 11.2 km/s (40,320 km/h) Required speed to reach space
Atmosphere 78% N₂, 21% O₂ 101.3 kPa standard pressure
Natural Radiation Shield Low (2.4 mSv/year) Shielded by geomagnetic field
Axial Tilt 23.44° Generates regular seasons
Sidereal Day 24.00 Hours One planetary rotation cycle
🌕 NATURAL SATELLITE

The Moon

0.165g (1/6th Gravity)

An airless, cratered world with an ancient basaltic and anorthositic crust. Devoid of wind and liquid water erosion, surface impacts and astronaut footprints remain pristine for millions of years.

Mean Radius 1,737 km (27.3% Earth) Approx. the width of Australia
Surface Gravity 1.62 m/s² (0.165g) You weigh 83.5% less here
Lunar Mass 7.34 × 10²² kg (1.2%) Relatively low celestial mass
Escape Velocity 2.38 km/s (8,570 km/h) Allows lighter departure stages
Atmosphere Near-Total Vacuum (Exosphere) 3 × 10⁻¹⁵ bar pressure
Space Radiation High (380–420 mSv/year) Requires shielded regolith habitats
Axial Tilt 1.54° Permanently shadowed polar craters
Synodic Day Length 29.53 Earth Days 14 Earth days day, 14 days night
